04 / FLAGSHIP R&D PROGRAM

Two intelligences.
One shared horizon.

In 42, Kael and an artificial intelligence learn to share perception, memory and experience—and negotiate the boundaries of a self. Project Janus takes that fictional premise as a research question: how might human and artificial intelligence learn from one another, expand human capability and preserve the freedom to choose?
